Niswas Nidra Wellness Clinic | Why Niswas Nidra?

One Clinic. Two Specialties. A Lifetime of Difference.

Most chest doctors stop at lungs. Most sleep doctors stop at counselling. Dr. Anirban Deb is one of the very few specialists in Eastern India formally trained in both — and the clinic is named to reflect that promise.

Lungs That Work

From asthma and COPD to advanced interventional procedures most hospitals don't even offer, our pulmonology care covers the full spectrum of breath.

Sleep That Restores

Snoring, sleep apnea, insomnia, daytime fatigue — diagnosed properly with in-lab and home polysomnography, treated with CPAP/BiPAP therapy tailored to you.

Globally Trained, Locally Available
